Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026 • Effective Date: November 1, 2026

This Cancellation and Refund Policy outlines the terms governing purchases, trial periods, and license activations for Pauzos (pauzos.com), a proprietary software product developed, owned, and operated by STRATEXA (Proprietorship), registered in Puducherry, India.

⚡ Key Policy Summary

We offer a 100% unrestricted 3-Day (72-hour) Free Trial with zero credit card required so you can thoroughly test all features on your workstation before purchasing. Because software licenses are delivered digitally and instantly, payments made after concluding the 3-day trial are non-refundable.

1. 3-Day Unrestricted Free Trial

We believe in absolute transparency and want you to be completely satisfied with your workstation productivity experience before spending any money:

  • Every user is entitled to an automatic, full-featured 3-Day (72-hour) free trial upon downloading and launching the application.
  • No credit card, debit card, or upfront financial commitment is required to start your trial.
  • All desktop capabilities—including recurring business day routines, wellness eye-break timers, Liquid Glass alerts, and immutable audit logs—are completely unlocked during the trial.
  • Following the 72-hour period, the application defaults to our permanent Freemium 2-routine limit, giving you ample time to decide whether to purchase the Pro Lifetime License.

2. No Refunds on Paid Licenses

Because Pauzos provides a full 3-day evaluation window prior to purchase, and because software licenses and digital binaries are irrevocably delivered immediately upon payment:

  • All sales are final: Once a Pro Lifetime License or digital key has been issued, payments are strictly non-refundable.
  • We do not offer retroactive refunds, partial refunds, or discretionary returns based on change of mind, lack of workstation usage, or user hardware obsolescence.
  • We encourage all customers to make full use of the 3-day free trial on their specific operating system (Windows, macOS, or Linux) to verify compatibility and workflow satisfaction prior to completing checkout.

3. Cancellation Policy

Depending on your chosen licensing tier:

  • Lifetime Licenses (One-Time Payment): Lifetime licenses (such as our … introductory launch tier) are single, one-time charges with no recurring renewals. There are no recurring subscriptions to cancel; your access remains active permanently.
  • Recurring Subscriptions (If applicable in future tiers): If you are subscribed to a recurring plan, you may cancel at any time directly through your account settings or by emailing support@pauzos.com at least 24 hours prior to the next billing date. Upon cancellation, you will retain access until the end of your prepaid billing period, and no further charges will be incurred.

4. Erroneous, Duplicate, or Failed Deductions

In the unlikely event of an electronic glitch or payment gateway network failure:

  • If your bank account or UPI handle was debited multiple times for a single license order, or debited without an automated license confirmation generated, please contact us within 7 days at support@pauzos.com with your 12-digit UPI Reference Number (UTR) or Gateway Transaction ID.
  • Verified duplicate charges will be reversed in full. Refunds are credited back to the original payment source (bank account, card, or UPI VPA) within 5 to 7 business days in compliance with Reserve Bank of India (RBI) payment settlement guidelines.

5. Multi-Device Reallocation & Technical Support

Each Pro license seat permits activation on up to 2 concurrent devices (e.g., your primary work laptop and a home desktop). If you purchase a new computer or format your hard drive:

  • You do not need to purchase a new license. Our licensing engine allows device deregistration and seat re-pairing.
  • For licensing transfer assistance or technical inquiries, our engineering support team is available at support@pauzos.com.
