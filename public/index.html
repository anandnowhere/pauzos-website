What exactly is Pauzos?+

Pauzos Personal Reminder Assistant is a desktop app for Windows, macOS, and Linux. You schedule a task and it shows a floating reminder above your open desktop apps. You can complete, snooze, or skip the reminder and review your local activity history.

What happens after the free trial?+

The trial lasts exactly 72 hours from first launch. Afterward, the first two routines you created stay active for free. Additional routines remain locked until you activate a lifetime license.

Does it work on my phone?+

Pauzos runs on desktop computers with Windows, macOS, or Linux. Use this page on your computer to download the matching edition. You can copy the page link from the download section.

Can I use it on a work laptop?+

Yes—Pauzos was designed and built specifically with corporate users and work laptops in mind. In Windows Portable mode, it runs directly from any local folder without requiring administrator privileges or a standard system installer. It writes no files to restricted root directories in the C: drive and does not install any background Windows Services, leaving no intrusive system traces that typical organization security scans flag or block.

For enterprise laptops and strictly managed workstations, we also provide the official Microsoft Store edition. Fully authenticated and certified by Microsoft, it runs inside secure Windows App containers—the modern golden standard for corporate desktops and IT compliance.

Your routines, notes, and activity history remain entirely private on your computer; reminder scheduling runs 100% locally. Get it on Microsoft Store ↗ or download the Windows Portable edition above.

Where are my reminders and notes stored?+

Your schedules, checklists, notes, and activity logs are stored locally on your computer. Online services support authentication, license and trial checks, pricing information, and updates. Read the privacy policy ↗

What is the difference between the Annual Pass and Lifetime Pro?+

The Annual Pass provides 1 full year of unlimited routines and updates for 1 workstation device with frictionless 1-click checkout (zero forced bank mandates) and gentle renewal reminders at Day 360.

The Lifetime Pro tier allows you to pay once and use Pauzos forever on 2 concurrent devices (e.g. your work PC + personal laptop) with all future updates included for life and zero recurring fees ever.
