Shipping & Digital Delivery Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026 • Effective Date: November 1, 2026

This Shipping and Delivery Policy outlines the delivery processes, fulfillment timelines, and electronic access terms for software products and Pro Lifetime Licenses purchased on Pauzos (pauzos.com), operated by STRATEXA (Proprietorship), registered in Puducherry, India.

⚡ 100% Digital Delivery Summary

Pauzos is a purely digital software product. There are no physical shipments, paper packages, or courier deliveries. All software binaries and Pro license credentials are delivered electronically and after successful payment verification and license activation.

1. Nature of Products & Delivery Method

All items sold on pauzos.com are digital productivity applications and software licenses:

  • Digital Executables & Installers: Cross-platform binaries for Windows (.exe / portable), macOS (.dmg), and Linux (.deb / .AppImage).
  • Pro Lifetime License Credentials: Account-based lifetime access using your purchase email, with two simultaneous logins.
  • Delivery Channels:
    1. Immediate On-Screen Display: Directly following successful payment verification, the checkout window provides your download links and license activation details.
    2. Automated Email Dispatch: A formal receipt and welcome email containing your account activation instructions, download links, and onboarding guides are simultaneously sent to the email address provided during checkout.

2. Fulfillment Timelines & Delivery SLA

Once the checkout page has verified your captured payment and activated your license:

  • Standard Fulfillment Time: Instant (typically within 1 to 5 minutes of transaction completion).
  • Maximum SLA Delivery Window: In the rare event of payment verification or email delivery delays, delivery may take up to 15 to 30 minutes.
  • If you have not received your activation email within 30 minutes, please check your spam/junk folder or contact our support team immediately.

Keep the payment page open until it confirms activation. If the connection is interrupted, reopen the payment page in the same browser and select “Check payment & activation again”. Do not pay again to resolve a delivery issue.

3. Shipping Charges & Physical Shipments

Because Pauzos distributes software exclusively via high-speed digital infrastructure:

  • Shipping Costs: ₹0.00 (Nil). We never charge shipping, handling, packaging, or postal freight fees.
  • Physical Delivery: We do not ship physical CD-ROMs, DVDs, USB drives, or physical merchandise. All software is obtained via secure electronic download.

4. Re-downloading & Perpetual Access

Purchasing a Pro Lifetime License entitles you to perpetual access to the software:

  • You may re-download software binaries at any time directly from our public download portal at pauzos.com/download.html without incurring any additional charges.
  • If you misplace your account access or switch to a new workstation, our customer support desk can re-issue your activation credentials upon verification of your registered purchase email address.
