Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 23, 2026 • Effective Date: October 23, 2026

At Pauzos (referred to as "we", "our", or "us", operated under Stratexa), your privacy is our foundational principle. We build desktop productivity software engineered to keep your data strictly on your personal device.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike cloud productivity apps, Pauzos is an offline-first local application:

  • We do not collect, upload, or sync your daily routines, tasks, calendar data, or personal notes to any cloud server.
  • Your application data is stored exclusively in a local SQLite database or encrypted file on your local computer hard drive.
  • We do not record your screen, keystrokes, or background application activity.

2. Information Collected During Purchase & Licensing

When you purchase a license or activate the application:

  • Transaction Information: We collect your email address and 12-digit UPI Reference (UTR) number solely to verify payment credit and deliver your license credentials. We do not store banking passwords or UPI PINs.
  • License Verification: To ensure single-device license compliance, the desktop application connects to our secure licensing database (Supabase) using a cryptographic hardware identifier (hash). No task contents, routines, or schedule details are ever transmitted during license checks.

3. Software Updates & Telemetry

Pauzos includes an integrated version checker that queries our public GitHub release repository to verify if a newer version is available. This query only checks the current version tag against the latest release and transmits zero identifiable personal task data.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your routine data resides 100% on your machine, you have total control over it. Uninstalling Pauzos or deleting its local data directory completely removes all your records permanently.
