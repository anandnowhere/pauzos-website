Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026 • Effective Date: November 1, 2026

At Pauzos (pauzos.com), owned and operated by STRATEXA (Proprietorship), your personal and workstation privacy is our core architectural foundation. We develop offline-first workstation software engineered specifically to ensure your daily routines and task records never leave your local hard drive.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike cloud software and web trackers, Pauzos is strictly an offline-first local application:

  • We do not collect, upload, or sync your daily routines, tasks, calendar reminders, compliance logs, or personal notes to any cloud server.
  • Your application data is stored locally in an encrypted JSON or SQLite file located strictly within your computer's user directory.
  • We never record your screen, log your keystrokes, or monitor background application processes.

2. Information Collected During Purchase & Payment Processing

When you purchase a Pauzos Pro Lifetime License:

  • Transaction Information: We collect your email address and transaction reference (such as the 12-digit UPI UTR number or Gateway Order ID) solely to verify payment settlement and dispatch your activation credentials.
  • Payment Processing Safety: All online payments are handled directly by PCI-DSS compliant and RBI-authorized payment gateway partners. STRATEXA does not capture, store, or have access to your credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, net banking credentials, or UPI PINs.
  • Hardware Fingerprinting: To enforce our 2-device concurrent seat policy, the application verifies activation using a one-way SHA-256 cryptographic hardware hash (derived from host architecture and network interface). This token contains zero readable personal files, documents, or names.

3. Software Updates & Telemetry

Pauzos contains an automated version checker querying our public GitHub release repository to verify whether an update is available. This check transmits only the current version string and client OS type. No user routine data or telemetry is attached.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your reminder and schedule database resides 100% on your local workstation, you maintain complete data sovereignty. Deleting the application or removing its local configuration directory permanently erases all your task records.
