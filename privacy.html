Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 23, 2026 • Effective Date: October 23, 2026

At Pauzos (referred to as "we", "our", or "us", operated under Stratexa), your privacy is our foundational principle. We build desktop productivity software engineered to keep your data strictly on your personal device.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike cloud productivity apps, Pauzos is an offline-first local application:

  • We do not collect, upload, or sync your daily routines, tasks, calendar data, or personal notes to any cloud server.
  • Your application data is stored exclusively in a local SQLite database or encrypted file on your local computer hard drive.
  • We do not record your screen, keystrokes, or background application activity.

2. Information Collected During Purchase

When you purchase a Pro license on our website (pauzos.com):

  • Transaction Information: We collect your name, email address, and billing information to generate your license and receipt.
  • Payment Processing: We do not store or process card numbers or UPI PINs directly. All transactions are securely handled by PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateways (such as Razorpay, Cashfree, or Stripe).

3. Software Updates & Telemetry

Pauzos includes an integrated version checker that queries our public GitHub release repository to see if a newer version is available. This query only checks the current version tag against the latest release and does not transmit any identifiable personal information.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your routine data resides 100% on your machine, you have total control over it. Uninstalling Pauzos or deleting its local data directory completely removes all your records permanently.

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please contact us at:
